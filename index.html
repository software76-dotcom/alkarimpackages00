MANUFACTURING / FORM IN MOTION

The machines.
Behind the craft.

From bonding and corrugation to the final cut. Explore the processes that turn paper and board into packaging.

01 / SHEET PASTING

Sheet pasting machine

An even adhesive layer brings sheets together, preparing bonded board for the next stage of box making.

Roller coatingSheet bonding
02 / CORRUGATION

Oblique corrugation machine

Fluted paper meets a liner to form single-face corrugated board—the structured layer at the heart of corrugated packaging.

Flute formationBoard structure
03 / CUTTING & CREASING

Rotary cutting & creasing

Rotating tools slit board and form crease lines, preparing sheets for accurate folding and box assembly.

Sheet sizingFold preparation
04 / DIE CUTTING

Platen die-cutting press

A shaped die cuts and creases board in a platen press, turning flat sheets into defined packaging blanks.

Shaped cuttingCreased blanks
